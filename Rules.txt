##Conditional Rules

The contract generator must apply the following conditional rules at PDF-generation time:

1. DRONE DIMENSIONS / WEIGHT
   If any selected item is in the drone category, include its dimensions (inches) and weight (grams) on its line in the equipment list. Non-drone items omit these.
   The borrower frequently needs the weight value to apply for insurance, so it is important that every commercial drone (the DJI fleet) prints with weight.
   EXCEPTION: Custom-built drones (currently CineKing 4K Micro Indoor, Cinewhoop, DIY Racing/Freestyle, and any future item in the inventory whose Weight in grams field is blank) print with dimensions only -- no weight line. This is intentional; we do not have authoritative weight values for custom builds. The script must omit the weight portion whenever the weightGrams field is absent on an inventory item.

2. OUT-OF-MASSACHUSETTS TRAVEL
   If the primary location is "Other" and the text does not contain "MA", "Massachusetts", or "Waltham", add (a) "You have permission to transport the item(s) to {location, other}" and (b) "If taking public transportation, we highly recommend traveling with the device in your carry-on luggage" to the loan-period paragraph.

3. DRONE CLAUSES
   If the loan includes a drone, append three sentences after the loan-period paragraph:
     - "I have obtained all necessary licenses and permits and insurance for my project as per the drone laws in {location of the loan}."
     - (only if the "Names of additional individuals" field is populated) "I acknowledge the following individuals will use the drone: {names}."
     - "Brandeis Design and Innovation is not liable for any damages incurred during this loan period, including if the drone is operated by another person."

4. DEPARTMENT CHAIR APPROVAL
   The Department Chair Certification paragraph and the Chair signature block must appear when EITHER of these conditions is true:
     (a) The loan includes the Artec scanner (or any other item flagged "requires chair approval" in the inventory configuration).
     (b) The Borrower Role is "Undergraduate student" or "Graduate student" AND the total replacement value of the selected items (quantity x replacement value, summed across all items) is at or above $1,000.
   The threshold for rule (b) is configurable in the script as STUDENT_LOAN_THRESHOLD; the current value is $1,000.

5. SIGNATURE BLOCK APPEARANCE
   Both the borrower signature block and the (conditional) department-chair signature block must render as thin horizontal LINES with small captions beneath each line -- NOT as filled boxes, gray rectangles, or large pre-filled blocks of text. The fields stay empty when the PDF is generated; the borrower (and chair, when applicable) fill them in directly in their PDF viewer.

6. CONTRACT CREATOR NAME ORDER
   The Contract Creator's name appears in the form/dropdown as "Last, First" for ease of alphabetical lookup. When the script writes the creator's name into the contract footer ("{Contract Creator} created this contract on {Contract Date}."), it must flip the order to "First Last". Names with middle initials should preserve them in the middle position (e.g., "Susmann, Natalie M." becomes "Natalie M. Susmann"; "Saludo, Gianna Crisha" becomes "Gianna Crisha Saludo").


7. NO AUTO-PLACED LENDER SIGNATURE
   The contract must NOT stamp an image of anyone's handwritten signature into
   the PDF. An earlier version embedded a signature JPEG in the page and drew it
   above the typed closing; that was removed because the page is intended to be
   hosted as a public web page, and a downloadable signature image is a forgery
   risk for whoever it belongs to.
   What the end of the contract carries instead:
     (a) the typed closing -- "Best," followed by the ORG signer name, title,
         and organisation;
     (b) the line "BDI Full Time Staff acknowledge this contract on
         {Contract Date}."
   The borrower signature block (and the chair block when triggered) is
   unaffected -- those stay as empty lines for people to sign by hand, per
   rule 5. If a countersignature is ever required, it is added by hand or by the
   signer's own PDF tool after generation, never baked into the template.


8. DS LAB SWIPE ACCESS GATE
   The generator opens on a gate screen, not on the form. It asks "Does this
   person have swipe access to the DS Lab?" with Yes / No buttons, and the rest
   of the form is not shown until it is answered:
     (a) "Yes" reveals the form and enables contract generation.
     (b) "No" keeps the form hidden and shows the warning: "Borrowers must have
         swipe access to the DS Lab. Please see https://makerlab.fyi/moodle to
         complete the training."
     (c) Unanswered (the initial state) also keeps the form hidden.
   Blocking is enforced three ways: the form is hidden, the Generate Contract
   PDF button is disabled, and the submit handler refuses and returns the user
   to the gate screen. Never rely on the hidden form or disabled button alone.
   Re-answering is non-destructive by design. A "Change the DS Lab access
   answer" link at the bottom of the form returns to the gate screen, and
   answering "Yes" again brings back a part-filled form untouched -- a student
   worker who mis-clicks "No" must not lose their work. Reset Form clears
   everything and returns to the gate.
   The answer is NOT printed on the contract. Every generated contract implies
   "Yes" by construction, so printing it would add nothing.
